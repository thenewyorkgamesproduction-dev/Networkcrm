# MVP Scope

## Purpose

Let Richard capture a person or relationship story in about ten seconds, retrieve the right people later, and maintain important relationships without losing context.

## Must work

1. Save a messy sentence, even with only a name, phone, or Instagram.
2. Preserve the original sentence as relationship history.
3. Append context to an existing person when phone, Instagram, email, or one unambiguous name matches.
4. Paste multiple people, one per line.
5. Search without AI usage.
6. Rank strong topic affinity above casual mentions.
7. Display copyable name, Instagram, and phone fields.
8. Create and complete follow-ups.
9. Track lightweight opportunities.
10. Keep API credentials out of browser code and GitHub.

## Not required for V1

- Semantic embeddings
- Automatic Instagram enrichment
- Apple Contacts sync
- Full iMessage ingestion
- Native iOS application
- Automatic AI calls on each action
- Advanced room chemistry or group balancing
